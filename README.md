# QUEEN MONIKA — 3D Royal Birthday Cinematic Experience (21 September)

A high-end, 100% code-driven WebGL 3D birthday celebration website created exclusively for Monika by Anish.

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Pure 3D Code (Three.js WebGL)**:
   - Procedural 3D Queen Model featuring a flowing modern haute-couture evening gown with real-time silk physics/wave animation.
   - 3D Golden Tiara/Crown adorned with sparkling rubies and diamonds.
   - Her actual portrait face seamlessly integrated into a glowing royal cameo bezel.
   - Full 360-degree interactive rotation (drag/swipe controls) on mobile and desktop.

2. **4 Distinct 3D Styles / Scenes**:
   - **Scene 1: राज्याभिषेक (The Coronation)** — Floating marble & gold pedestal, falling 3D velvet rose petals, interactive crown coronation.
   - **Scene 2: आधुनिक स्टाइल (Haute Couture Runway)** — High-fashion reflective runway, glowing neon light arches, moving studio spotlights.
   - **Scene 3: यादों का कहकशां (3D Photo Galaxy)** — Central pulsing crystal 3D heart with 4 orbiting 3D golden frames showcasing all 4 photos of Monika (with auto-orientation for `monika-4.jpg`).
   - **Scene 4: जन्मदिन जश्न (3D Cake & Hindi Love Letter)** — Multi-tier royal birthday cake with real flickering 3D candles. Interactive candle-blowing ceremony that triggers grand 3D fireworks, floating balloons, and screen confetti!

3. **Romantic Hindi Music System**:
   - Built-in Web Audio API polyphonic Bollywood romantic piano synthesizer (Dm-Bb-C-F romantic chord progression) that plays with zero network latency.
   - Music controls (Play / Pause, Animated Equalizer visualizer, curated links for YouTube playback of Kesariya, Tum Hi Ho, Raatan Lambiyan, Apna Bana Le).

4. **Anti-Sadness Heartfelt Hindi Love Letter**:
   - Specifically written to comfort her habit of feeling sad on her birthday and remind her how cherished, unique, and magnificent she is as the ultimate Queen.

## 🚀 How to Deploy to Netlify

1. Open [Netlify](https://app.netlify.com/).
2. Drag and drop the `birthday_movie_monika` folder into the Netlify deploy area.
3. The site is instantly live with high performance!
